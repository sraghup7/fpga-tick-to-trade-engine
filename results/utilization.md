**DSP usage is 0 for the whole design, classifier included** — this is a
placeholder-classifier artifact, not a demonstrated DSP budget: `model/`'s
weights are the documented S4 placeholder (`w_i=1`, bias=0, see CLAUDE.md /
`docs/design_decisions.md`), so Vivado maps the int8x8 products to LUTs and
trivially reduces the multiply-by-one classifier away entirely. The `<=8 DSP`
classifier budget (S7.3) is met vacuously until a real trained model lands (S4).

**`csr_block.v` is the single largest hand-written contributor to LUT usage**
— 2,335 LUTs / 2,253 FFs, 11.2% of the part's entire LUT fabric, measured via
`report_utilization -hierarchical -hierarchical_depth 2` on the routed D40
netlist (`docs/design_decisions.md` D41). Root cause and a proposed fix (a
shared-incrementer counter file + pipelined read mux, ~1,500+ LUTs recoverable)
are documented there, not yet implemented.
