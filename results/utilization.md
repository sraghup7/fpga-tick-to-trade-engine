**DSP usage is 0 for the whole design, classifier included.** Do not assume
this means untrained placeholder weights (`w_i=1`) -- check
`docs/design_decisions.md`'s latest D-entry for the model's actual status.
Vivado maps the classifier's int8x8 constant multiplies to LUT fabric rather
than DSP48 slices at this optimization setting regardless of whether the
weights are the placeholder or a real trained model (small constant multiplies
are cheap enough in LUTs that Vivado doesn't need a DSP for them here). The
`<=8 DSP` classifier budget (S7.3) is met either way, but this line alone
cannot tell you which case you're looking at.

**`csr_block.v` is the single largest hand-written contributor to LUT usage**
— 2,335 LUTs / 2,253 FFs, 11.2% of the part's entire LUT fabric, measured via
`report_utilization -hierarchical -hierarchical_depth 2` on the routed D40
netlist (`docs/design_decisions.md` D41). Root cause and a proposed fix (a
shared-incrementer counter file + pipelined read mux, ~1,500+ LUTs recoverable)
are documented there, not yet implemented.
